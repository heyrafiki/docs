Heyrafiki connects Care and payment operations through shared resource identifiers and versioned contracts.

## Resource model

| Resource | Purpose |
| --- | --- |
| Practitioners and availability | Discover synthetic profiles and recurring hours |
| Bookings and Sessions | Link scheduled Care to its lifecycle record |
| Eligibility and pre-authorization | Record payer decisions before covered Care |
| Claims and remittance | Submit delivered services and reconcile payer funds |
| Webhooks | Receive signed notifications when resource state changes |

## Covered Care, end to end

<Steps>
  <Step title="Discover">
    List Practitioners, retrieve one profile and read recurring availability.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check Cover">
    Create an eligibility check. Where the payer requires authorization, bind a pre-authorization request to that decision.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Book">
    Create a Booking with the selected Practitioner, time, format and payment source.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Deliver">
    The Booking and Session identifiers keep the scheduled service linked to its lifecycle record.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Claim and reconcile">
    Submit the delivered service, record Claim decisions and allocate remittance against the approved amount.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Financial references

Eligibility, pre-authorization, Booking, Session, Claim and remittance identifiers stay linked across the workflow. See [Resources](/resources) for money representation and identifier rules.
