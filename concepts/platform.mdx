Heyrafiki is Mental Healthcare Infrastructure. Finding Care, running a Practice, managing Benefits and handling Payments are connected parts of one Platform.

## Product surfaces

| Surface | Primary responsibility |
| --- | --- |
| People | Find and fund Care, manage Sessions and control sharing |
| Practitioners | Run a Practice, communicate with Clients and manage revenue |
| Organizations | Fund Programs and review aggregate participation |
| Insurers | Operate Claims and Practitioner Networks |
| Developers | Extend approved Heyrafiki capabilities as access becomes available |

## Human-First intelligence

Iris supports Matchmaking, scheduling, Scribe drafts and product guidance. Clinical Notes remain drafts until a Practitioner approves them.

## One connected experience

People control their Care choices. Practitioners manage their Practice. Organizations and Insurers work with reviewed aggregate or operational information that matches their role. Private Session content stays outside those reporting views.
