---
title: "Data ownership"
description: "Place each fact in one authoritative store and move projections safely."
---

One business fact has one authoritative store. Other stores may hold references or projections, but they do not create a second source of truth.

## Planned ownership

| Domain | Authority |
| --- | --- |
| Enterprise identity | WorkOS |
| Conversations, Scheduling, Tasks and live application state | Convex |
| Approved clinical records | Postgres |
| Claims, ledger entries and reconciliation | Postgres |
| Files and media | S3-compatible object storage |
| Product telemetry | PostHog, without clinical or Conversation content |

Supabase is one possible managed Postgres provider. The provider decision must consider residency, latency, restoration, security controls, operating cost and portability before installation.

## Cross-store projections

Cross-store movement uses versioned events:

1. Commit the authoritative change and its outbox record together.
2. Publish a versioned event.
3. Record the event in the consumer inbox.
4. Apply the projection idempotently.
5. Retry with backoff and move exhausted work to a reviewable state.
6. Reconcile projections with the authority after incidents and on a schedule.

## Embeddings

An embedding stays with the authoritative data it represents. Store its source reference, source version, model, dimensions, Consent class and deletion path. Revoking or deleting the source must revoke or delete the derived vector through the same policy boundary.
