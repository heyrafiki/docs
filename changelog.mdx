<Update label="25 July 2026" description="Platform model, Session flow and Iris" tags={["Platform", "Clinical", "Payments"]}>
  The Platform model is now public. Heyrafiki is built from six objects — Person, Practitioner, Session, Note, Claim and Ledger entry — with a defined Session flow from Match to Settle. See the [Platform overview](/concepts/platform).

  **New**
  - **Iris matching.** Iris filters verified Practitioners by language, focus, budget, schedule and Cover, then ranks and explains her picks so the Person chooses.
  - **Hey Video Sessions.** Sessions run in a Room created with the booking, in Hey Video or in person. If Iris joins as Scribe, the Client is notified in the Room.
  - **SOAP Notes with Practitioner approval.** Iris drafts Notes in SOAP structure; nothing enters the record until a Practitioner approves.
  - **Escrowed self-pay.** Self-paying Clients pay at booking. Heyrafiki holds funds and releases to the Practitioner the moment the Session ends.
  - **Coded Claims from approved Notes.** Covered Sessions generate a Claim from the approved Note, checked before submission to the Payer.
  - **FHIR and ICD-10, DHIS2-aligned exports.** Clinical records follow FHIR shapes with ICD-10 coding; aggregate exports align with national reporting including DHIS2.

  **Updates**
  - **Role boundaries enforced in the data model.** Organizations and Funders see aggregate reporting only. Insurers see Claim evidence, not Note content. Identity data is stored apart from clinical data. Details in [Security and Privacy](/security/overview).
  - **API and SDK scope confirmed.** The API and SDKs open with the Platform. Sandbox keys go to the [Waitlist](https://heyrafiki.space/waitlist) first.
</Update>
