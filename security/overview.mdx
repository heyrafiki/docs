title: "Security and Privacy"
description: "Heyrafiki's Privacy-first principles for Mental Healthcare data: role and purpose-based access, Consent, transport protections and clinical boundaries."
---

Heyrafiki is Privacy-first Mental Healthcare Infrastructure. Access follows a person's role, the purpose of the request and the Consent attached to it.

## Core principles

- Collect only what a workflow needs.
- Separate identity information from clinical information.
- Keep sensitive access scoped and reviewable.
- Let People control optional sharing.
- Keep private Care content out of product analytics.

## In transit

Every response is served over HTTPS with HSTS. Browsers are told to refuse framing, ignore MIME sniffing, send a lean referrer and keep device permissions limited to what a Session needs.

## Clinical boundaries

Iris may draft Clinical Notes, but a Practitioner must review and approve them before they become part of the approved record.

## Organization access

Organizations receive aggregate reporting for the Programs they fund. Those reports never include a Person's identity, diagnosis, Session content or private Conversation.

## Product intelligence

Iris supports product guidance and Practitioner-reviewed drafts. Private Session content is not used to train product models.
